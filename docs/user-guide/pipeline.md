# Tracking your applications

Your applications live on the Dashboard (`/dashboard`), under **Your applications**. It lists every
application you have sent, independent of the resume behind it. (The old Pipeline page and its board
are gone; `/pipeline` now takes you to the Dashboard.)

## The list

Five filters sit above the list: **Applied · Interviewing · Offer · Closed · All**, each with a
count. A job in Screening counts under Applied. Each row shows the company, the role, the date of
its last status change, and a badge in plain words, such as "Applied", "Screening", "Interviewing",
"Offer", "Rejected" or "Position filled". The newest change is first, and the list shows ten rows
until you choose **Show all**.

## The application sheet

Selecting a row opens its sheet: the status, a notes box, the status history, a link to the resume
that was used, and a **Delete** button that asks before it removes the application.

## Moving a status forward

The sheet's **Status** control moves an application through **Applied → Screening → Interviewing →
Offer** (or straight to Closed). Each change is saved immediately and recorded in the history below.

## Closing with a reason

Once an application's status is set to **Closed**, a second control appears for the reason, so a
closed application always says why:

- **Rejected** — the employer turned you down.
- **Withdrew** — you pulled out of the process.
- **No response** — the employer went quiet.
- **Position filled** — the role was filled by someone else, or pulled entirely.

## Follow-ups

The sheet has a date field for a follow-up. Setting one and pressing **Save follow-up** saves the
date with the application.

## Notes and history

A free-text notes box saves independently of the status ("Save notes"), and every status change,
including the very first, appears in the history list with its date.
